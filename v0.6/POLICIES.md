## 📴 Offline Usage Policy

### 7-Day Offline Grace Period

**How It Works:**
1. App validates license online when connected to internet
2. After validation, app works **fully offline for 7 consecutive days**
3. On **day 8**, app requires internet connection to re-validate
4. After successful validation, another 7-day offline period begins
5. Cycle repeats indefinitely

**What Happens on Day 8 Without Internet:**
- App displays: "Please connect to internet to validate your license"
- All features locked until internet connection restored
- No data loss - all tournament data preserved
- Instant unlock after reconnection and validation

**Exception - Lifetime Licenses:**
- Unlimited offline use
- No 7-day restriction
- Validation still recommended for updates

**Perfect For:**
- Venues with unreliable WiFi
- Outdoor tournaments
- Remote locations
- Backup connectivity issues
