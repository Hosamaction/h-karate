Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before getting started

What operating systems work with H Karate?

H Karate runs on Windows 10 and Windows 11 (64-bit). macOS and Linux versions are in development and coming soon. Contact us if you need early access to beta versions.

Does it really work offline?

Absolutely. H Karate is built offline-first. You only need internet for license activation and a periodic check every 7 days. If you're at a tournament without WiFi, the app continues working for up to a week. Perfect for venues with unreliable connectivity.

How do wireless judges connect?

Start the judge server from the home screen. A QR code appears on your screen. Judges scan it with any phone or tablet browser on the same WiFi network. No app download, no login, no hassle. They're judging within 30 seconds.

Can I use my license on multiple computers?

Monthly and Yearly licenses work on one machine. The Lifetime plan includes 2 machine activations. Need to transfer to a new computer? Just contact support with your Machine ID and we'll handle it immediately.

How do I set up the livestream overlay?

Start the judge server, then copy the OBS URL from Settings → Appearance. Add it as a Browser Source in OBS Studio with a transparent background. The overlay displays live scores, timer, and winner announcements — all updating in real time with zero latency.

Which languages are supported?

Full interface translation in English, Arabic (with complete RTL layout), Japanese, French, German, and Spanish. Switch instantly from Settings → General. Need another language? Request it and we'll add it in the next update.

What's your cancellation policy?

You can cancel monthly or yearly subscriptions anytime. Your service continues until the end of your paid period. Contact support@hkarate.app to process cancellation. Lifetime licenses never expire.

Is my tournament data sent to the cloud?

Never. All match history, competitor profiles, and settings are stored 100% locally on your machine. Nothing is uploaded to any server. You own your data completely. We can't access it even if we wanted to.

How fast is support response time?

Email support responds within 48 hours for Monthly plans, 24 hours for Yearly, and 12 hours for Lifetime. But we usually reply much faster. Phone support available 10 hours daily for urgent tournament-day issues.

Can I try it before buying?

Yes! Every plan includes a 14-day free trial with full features — no credit card required. Download, activate with a trial license, and test it at your next tournament. Upgrade when you're ready.
